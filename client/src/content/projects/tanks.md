Tanks! is a decompiled and rebuilt version of the Tanks minigame from Wii Play, made with web technology. It was one of my favorite games as a kid, and one day I wanted to scratch the itch to play it again.

I sat down with Claude to decompile the original ROM, extract its assets and gameplay logic, and rebuild the game with three.js. Once I had a faithful reconstruction of the original, I optimized it for high-resolution, responsive screens, added web-native menus, and extended it with new ways to play: online multiplayer, solo and team arenas, battle royale and elimination. We also added new enemy tanks, power-ups and artillery.

The result is a reimagined Tanks!: an even more addictive online MMO, hosted on my homelab cluster.
