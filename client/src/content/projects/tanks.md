Toy tanks on a wooden table, shells that bounce off the walls once, and mines you lay behind you. This is a faithful port of a classic minigame to the browser. All 100 missions play exactly as they always did, because the game logic is ported line by line rather than rebuilt from memory. Nothing from the original disc ships with the code. The models, levels and sounds are converted at build time from your own copy.

On top of that there is everything the original never had. You can play the campaign online with a friend, in co-op or head to head. A level editor lets you build missions and share them with everyone else. The Arena is one huge map with up to 40 tanks and four modes (free-for-all, teams, battle royale and elimination), with power-ups, homing flares, shields and one nuke per match.

The game runs on three.js with WebGPU. Online play is server-authoritative: a Bun server runs the simulation and streams snapshots, and the client predicts your own tank so it stays responsive. Every push to main is checked against replays of the campaign and deployed as a new version by GitHub Actions.
